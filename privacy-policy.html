Privacy Policy

App: Water Sort Puzzle · Developer: WaterSort Studio
Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Water Sort Puzzle ("Application" or "App") handles user information when installed and used on your device.

1. Information We Process

Water Sort Puzzle is a 100% offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location data).

All gameplay progress (including completed levels, highest level reached, sound settings, and haptic preferences) is saved 100% locally on your device using Android's native SharedPreferences mechanism.

2. Network & Third-Party Services

The Application does not require or use Internet access permissions (android.permission.INTERNET). The current version contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, no in-app purchases (IAP), and no user tracking or analytics frameworks.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full control over your local data. You can erase all saved progress at any time by navigating to your device settings: Settings → Apps → Water Sort Puzzle → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage, or simply by uninstalling the Application from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Water Sort Puzzle is a wholesome, family-friendly color sorting puzzle game intended for users of all ages. Because the Application collects no personal data whatsoever, parents and guardians can feel confident allowing children to play the game safely.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future feature enhancements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top.
